Local SEO Tips for Home Service Businesses

A homeowner's water heater fails on a cold morning. They search "water heater repair near me" and call the first company that looks trustworthy. Local SEO is what decides whether that company is yours or the one three results below it.

Why fresh content helps

Google favors pages that answer real questions instead of just listing a phone number. A page that explains what a service actually involves, how long it takes, and what it costs on average gives Google more to work with and gives the homeowner a reason to trust you. Read more about SEO content for home services.

What to look at first

  • Is your Google Business Profile complete, with the correct category?
  • Does every service have its own page instead of one general page?
  • Have you replied to your last five reviews?
